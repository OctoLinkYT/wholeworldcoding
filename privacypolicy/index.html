Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

wholeworldcoding ("wholeworldcoding", "we", "us", or "our") operates websites, applications, and other projects intended for experimentation, learning, and general use.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on the particular wholeworldcoding service you use.

For accounts, this may include:

  • Your name or username
  • Your email address
  • A unique account identifier
  • Information necessary to authenticate your account

If you choose to sign in using Google or another supported identity provider, we may receive basic account information provided by that provider.

2. Google Account Information

Some wholeworldcoding applications may allow you to sign in using your Google account.

When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive information such as your name, email address, profile information, and a unique Google account identifier, depending on the permissions requested by the application.

Applications may request additional Google permissions when they need access to additional Google services. We only use information obtained through those permissions for the purpose described when the permission is requested.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide and operate our applications
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Communicate with users when necessary
  • Improve our services

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may use third-party services to operate portions of our applications, such as authentication, hosting, analytics, storage, or infrastructure. Those services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect our services, or prevent abuse.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the applicable service, maintain security, or satisfy legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we may delete or anonymize it.

6. Account Deletion

If you want your wholeworldcoding account or associated personal information deleted, contact us using the contact method provided by the relevant application or website.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate technical, security, or legal reasons.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by our services. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Important: wholeworldcoding projects may be experimental or independently developed. Do not submit highly sensitive information to a service unless that service specifically indicates that it is designed to handle such information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to one of our services, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

Questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy can be directed to wholeworldcoding through the contact information provided on our website.
